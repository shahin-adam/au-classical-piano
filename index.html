AU Classical Piano Uploader

AU Classical Piano Uploader is a private publishing tool used only by the channel owner. It uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's own finished videos to the owner's own channels, set their thumbnails, add them to the owner's playlists, and confirm that each video published correctly. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
